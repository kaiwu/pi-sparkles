# pi_sparkles_cn_setup

Experimental isolated mainland-China preflight. It registers `/cn-setup`,
`/cn-sources`, `cn_capabilities`, `cn_authorities`, and `cn_provider_health`.

Results visibly carry `track: "cn"`, CNY, `Asia/Shanghai`, `zh-CN`, and a
versioned `trackContext`. The plugin reports the installed pure identity,
calendar, rules, document/attachment, accounting, and per-family 85%
multi-channel coverage foundations while keeping every provider-backed
capability blocked until its source and rights are approved or its exact
track-owned tool is installed. The threshold does not make an unapproved
provider count toward readiness.

The optional `cn_disclosures` companion advances two surfaces to Experimental
when its tools are active: `cn_security_search` provides ambiguity-preserving
CNINFO catalogue candidates, and `cn_disclosure_search` provides catalogue-
bound announcement discovery with exact PDF identities. Neither proves an
exchange venue or redistribution rights, and accounting remains blocked.

The optional `cn_market_calendar` companion advances the authoritative-calendar
surface when `cn_market_calendar` is active. It requires an exact SSE, SZSE, or
BSE venue and exposes source-reviewed 2026 coverage, sessions, closures, and
limitations. It does not satisfy settlement, Connect, or a later-year calendar.

The optional `cn_market_data` companion advances quote and raw-history surfaces
only when both `cn_stock_quote` and `cn_stock_history` are active. It uses the
shared bounded Eastmoney public-web adapter for explicit SSE/SZSE/BSE six-digit
identities. It preserves provider scaling and raw unadjusted daily-bar lexemes;
latency, production service level, suspension completeness, adjustment factors,
and redistribution remain unknown.

The optional `cn_market_rules` companion advances the effective-rules surface
when `cn_trading_rules` is active. Its source-reviewed slice begins 2026-07-06
and covers only established, normally traded CNY A-shares for exact SSE Main,
SSE STAR, SZSE Main, SZSE ChiNext, and BSE pairs. Exceptional regimes,
settlement, eligibility, and computed order prices remain outside the profile.

The source view identifies CSRC, SSE, SZSE, BSE, CNINFO, and the Ministry of
Finance CAS/XBRL surfaces with official HTTPS links, exact roles, operational
access state, redistribution state, and limitations. A verified authority is
not reported as an unrestricted machine provider. The three exact
`finance_csrc` raw-HTML paths and exact, already-known `finance_cninfo` PDF paths
are marked `public_read_only_snapshot`; both remain no-redistribution and have
no semantic decoder. Exact PDFs receive bounded real-parser structural page
inspection, but no text-layer or OCR claim. CNINFO public search and all other
public/search surfaces remain unreviewed. A CNINFO repository artifact does not
prove an SSE/SZSE/BSE origin without separate official metadata.

No HK, SEC, OpenFIGI, or generic tool can satisfy a CN capability. The plugin
performs no network, environment, filesystem, or secret access, and unknown
providers remain `unknown` rather than healthy.
