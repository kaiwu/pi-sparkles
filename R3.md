# R3 — Provider policy and external implementation decisions

[Previous: R2 delivery](R2.md) · [Roadmap index](ROADMAP.md)

This phase records provider candidates and accepted narrow seams, rights,
source cautions, and external implementation selections. Provider choice
remains explicit and track-scoped; nothing here weakens the global rules or
course-demand gates in `ROADMAP.md`.

Finance-domain behavior, Pi architecture and professional acceptance are now
governed by [`PRODUCT_READINESS.md`](PRODUCT_READINESS.md) and tutor Sessions
40–46. The items below are provider/access/engineering selections or evidence
to obtain, not unanswered product-design questions.

## Provider notes

Provider choice is configuration and packaging policy, not an invisible
fallback chain.

- [SEC EDGAR APIs](https://www.sec.gov/search-filings/edgar-application-programming-interfaces)
  provide unauthenticated submissions and XBRL JSON, update throughout the day,
  and require compliant automated-access behavior.
- [OpenFIGI v3](https://www.openfigi.com/api/documentation) maps third-party
  identifiers and exposes explicit rate limits. Its v2 API reached sunset in
  July 2026, so new code must target v3.
- [FRED and ALFRED](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/docs/api/fred/series/series_observations.html)
  provide macro observations, transformations, release data, and historical
  vintages. Underlying series can retain third-party usage restrictions.
- [Alpaca market data](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/about-market-data-api)
  covers historical/real-time equities, options, and crypto over HTTP and
  WebSocket, but access and feed breadth depend on authentication and plan.
- [Alpaca historical stock bars](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/reference/stockbars)
  is the accepted first US OHLCV acquisition seam. The implemented slice fixes
  `1Day`, USD, ascending, and raw adjustment; requires explicit IEX/SIP and
  symbol-as-of identity; and follows page tokens only within caller budgets.
- [Alpaca corporate actions](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/reference/corporateactions-1)
  is the accepted rank-12 US action seam. The implemented slice fixes
  `region=us`, exact symbol/CUSIP, explicit process-date/data-quality/type
  filters, ascending order, and caller page/action budgets. It preserves exact
  rate lexemes, nullable fields, identity transitions, duplicates, and page
  evidence without treating processing date as announcement/effective date,
  empty currency as USD, or retrieval as completeness.
- [Alpaca paper trading](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/v1.4.2/docs/paper-trading)
  is useful for workflow testing but documents important simulation omissions.
- [IBKR Web API](https://ibkrcampus.com/campus/ibkr-api-page/webapi-doc/)
  exposes account, portfolio, market-data, and order workflows with authentication,
  session, subscription, and endpoint pacing constraints.
- [Nasdaq Data Link](https://docs.data.nasdaq.com/docs/getting-started) offers
  free and premium datasets through several APIs; datasets must retain their
  individual entitlement and usage terms.
- [CFTC Commitments of Traders](https://www.cftc.gov/MarketReports/CommitmentsofTraders/index.htm)
  is available through the CFTC public reporting API and is naturally suited to
  a positioning plugin.
- [ECB Data Portal](https://data.ecb.europa.eu/help/api/data) exposes SDMX data,
  including reference FX series and revision-aware queries.
- [FINRA Query API](https://developer.finra.org/products/query-api) covers
  equity and fixed-income datasets, while data-specific terms and TRACE product
  boundaries need separate review.
- [Coinbase Advanced Trade API](https://docs.cdp.coinbase.com/api-reference/advanced-trade-api/rest-api/introduction)
  provides REST order management and WebSocket market data for a possible
  crypto adapter; trading remains outside the early equity roadmap.
- [Shanghai Stock Exchange data services](https://english.sse.com.cn/markets/dataservice/products/)
  distinguish licensed real-time Level 1/2 products from historical and
  end-of-day data, so public visibility must not be mistaken for redistribution
  permission.
- [Shenzhen Stock Exchange data services](https://www.szse.cn/English/services/dataServices/index.html)
  describe real-time, delayed, end-of-day, corporate, and other information
  products managed by its authorized information company.
- [CNInfo](https://www.cninfo.com.cn/) is operated by an SZSE subsidiary as a
  statutory disclosure platform and aggregates Shanghai, Shenzhen, and Beijing
  company announcements and related public information.
- [Beijing Stock Exchange disclosures](https://www.bse.cn/disclosure/announcement.html)
  provide an official announcement surface that must be covered alongside the
  longer-established Shanghai and Shenzhen venues.
- [HKEX Stock Connect eligibility](https://www.hkex.com.hk/Mutual-Market/Stock-Connect/Eligible-Stocks/View-All-Eligible-Securities?sc_lang=en)
  publishes dated northbound/southbound eligibility and special sell-only lists;
  eligibility must therefore be modelled as time-varying state.
- [HKEX Securities Attribute Daily Files](https://data.hkex.com.hk/catalog/dataset/b3ce155a763811ef953342323a43f021)
  are the conditional first historical-membership source for exact XHKG only.
  The public dossier pins dataset code `SSD`, data asset `HKEXSADF`, historical
  SKU `SSD-SP-M`, daily Main Board/GEM snapshots, history from 2013-09-01, and
  the specification effective 2026-06-01. The current catalog reports 3,341
  files but exposes no machine-readable schema or completeness contract. The
  PDF specification defines 11 zipped CSV members and a positional equity
  schema; repeated `FILLER` headers and historical encoding changes require
  dated schema selection. `STKCODE` can be reused, while
  `STK_ID` survives name/code changes but can cover multiple stock codes, so it
  is not an exact listing/counter key alone. HKEX's
  [SFTP guide](https://www.hkex.com.hk/services/market-data-services/historical-data-services/-/media/1BC625C24564466B83E98212152F8EF7.ashx)
  documents public-key entitlement, exact path
  `/SSD/HKEX-DAILY-SA/HKEX/YYYY/MM/DD`, and a 14-day retrieval window. The
  [Data Marketplace terms](https://data.hkex.com.hk/terms) prohibit
  reproduction and redistribution; the product page and checkout expose that
  same version-1 terms surface rather than a public SKU-specific grant. The
  public documents do not define correction/completeness semantics or grant
  fixture/derived-output rights. This remains a conditional source, not an
  implemented or licensed seam.
- [CSRC disclosure rules](https://www.csrc.gov.cn/csrc/c106256/c1653948/content.shtml)
  and exchange rules should be retained with effective dates; summaries and
  translations cannot replace the controlling Chinese source.
- [National Bureau of Statistics data](https://data.stats.gov.cn/) is a
  candidate primary source for the China macro plugin, alongside PBOC and SAFE
  releases whose access and reuse terms must be studied per dataset.
- [CAPCO listed-company industry-classification results](https://www.capco.org.cn/xhgg/hyfl/hyfljg/202604/20260403/j_2026040315001700017751997384265508.html)
  are the accepted rank-17 mainland classification seam. The implemented slice
  pins the exact 2025-H2 PDF bytes and keeps the guideline effective date,
  result-period label, publication date, and retrieval instant separate. The
  artifact publishes stock code but no MIC or per-company validity interval;
  its 门类, 大类, and manufacturing-only 次类 are not mapped to other
  taxonomies. CAPCO attribution and no-redistribution limits remain explicit.

Unofficial scraping endpoints should not be the default data substrate. If a
community plugin supports one, it must identify it as unofficial, isolate its
decoder, respect the site's terms, and fail clearly when the surface changes.

## T5 blocker exits — 2026-08-12

All three T5 exits have named, repeatable paths and require no production
credential or provider matrix before building the declared global/multi-asset
journey.

- **`T5-PROVIDER-MATRIX` resolved:** every new T5 source, calculation and
  composition shell may consume one bounded caller-owned, versioned JSON
  import through `finance_local_import`, with an expected SHA-256 content hash,
  explicit source/provider identity, retrieval time, entitlement, licence,
  coverage, correction state and upstream receipts. This is the supported
  repeatable import path from Session 46, not an ad hoc pasted fixture and not
  origin authentication. `pi_macro_fred` retains its existing explicit
  caller-owned `FRED_API_KEY` adapter and scripted `finance_http` transport
  path. Additional providers remain focused conformance breadth.
- **`T5-REFERENCE-DATA` resolved:** the first acceptance data is a
  rights-safe scripted/import corpus pinned to the precise instrument and
  source contracts in Sessions 32 and 34–39. It supplies dated fund/NAV,
  Treasury, bond, convertible, option, X-CBT future, CFTC, crypto, official CN
  macro/policy and ECB reference-rate facts with source receipts and explicit
  fixture rights. Hashes bind bytes and handoffs but are never represented as
  exchange, agency, provider or authority signatures. Unknown correction,
  completeness and redistribution facts remain visible and restrict claims.
- **`T5-MULTI-ASSET-JOURNEY` resolved:** Sessions 40, 43, 45 and 46 define one
  global/cross-market professional task: identify separately labelled
  instrument legs; acquire/import exact evidence; run only caller-selected
  fund, fixed-income, convertible, option, future/COT, crypto, macro and FX
  calculations; then compose macro and market views under explicit alignment
  and native-currency policies. No synthetic global track, silent FX, index/
  ETF equivalence, provider substitution, model selection, interpretation,
  recommendation or trade effect is permitted.

T5 may therefore enter `building`. Its only acceptance lane remains
`test/tiers/t5_multi_asset_researcher`; CN-specific source legs receive focused
laws inside that one journey rather than separate CN/HK/US product variants.

owns a stable typed provider capability and focused adapter conformance suite;
credentials remain caller-owned environment inputs. Unknown service level,
licence, redistribution, correction or completeness facts stay visible and
restrict claims, but do not prevent local implementation and testing when a

  receipts that satisfy the day-workbench intraday data, auction/halt,
  freshness, sequence, entitlement, fixture, and redistribution contract
  without overstating delayed or snapshot data?
- Which licensed China market-data provider can cover Shanghai, Shenzhen, and

None of these choices changes the safety-first ordering: identity and
provenance, then primary-source research, then analytics and portfolio work,
then local simulation, non-executable external handoff, and read-only receipt
reconciliation. Broker order mutation is not a later project; it is outside the
repository's plugin scope.

### T6 external-provider boundary amendment — resolved 2026-08-14

This later decision supersedes the earlier statements in this file that kept
`T6-INTRADAY-PROVIDERS` open or prohibited public T6 implementation. The
delivered plugins are not provider distributions: OpenD, Futu direct API,
Alpaca, IBKR, every other gateway or SDK, credentials, entitlements, licences,
and live-provider certification are explicit caller-owned runtime dependencies.
They are neither shipped nor silently invoked by this repository. Consequently,
live access to three provider accounts is not a pre-build or packaging gate for
the provider-neutral plugin core.

The blocker exit is instead exact and testable inside the product boundary. A
caller must name the external capability and supply a bounded normalized packet
with exact `cn`, `hk`, or `us` identity, MIC, feed, entitlement, coverage,
licence, clocks, conditions, sequence lexemes, raw-content receipts, and all
unknown or conflicting facts. `finance_tape`, its stream state machine, and the
`stock_tape` Pi shell deterministically validate sequence gaps/resets,
correction/cancel lineage, clock deltas, condition coverage, duplicate/conflict
states, recovery outcomes, paging, cancellation, and receipts using rights-safe
fixtures and scripted transports. They do not authenticate the provider or turn
a provider declaration into exchange authority. The separately labelled CN,
HK, and US acceptance legs prove these shared laws independently; none supplies
or relabels another.

The earlier OpenD and direct-Futu probes remain useful development evidence,
but this resolution does not convert them into a runtime dependency or a claim
of provider completeness, MIC authentication, licensing, redistribution rights,
or production certification. Provider-specific conformance belongs to the
explicit external dependency chosen by the caller. Plugin load, packaging, and
acceptance remain network-free, credential-free, SDK-free, and OpenD-free.

Under this controlling boundary, the two README-only proposals are now real
packages and the seven former `track_partial` packages expose their complete
provider-neutral contracts: named transaction-tape possible-fill simulation,
external paper-receipt review, explicit read-only broker capability packets,
content-bound non-executable handoff and receipt reconciliation, and typed
compound compliance evaluation/explanation/version comparison. No plugin can
place, route, cancel, replace, or otherwise mutate an order.
